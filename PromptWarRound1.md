# Prompt War: Round 1 (Messy to Meaning)

## The Setup

You have exactly **15 minutes**, your wits,
and total access to any AI model or platform available to you. 

The goal of this round is simple but brutal: **Depth and robustness beat guesswork.**
You are competing individually.
An AI Agent will act as our automated gatekeeper,
parsing your submission through the Power App the second the buzzer sounds. 

Only the **Top 8 individual prompt engineering masters** will survive this sprint and advance to the final Round 2. 

---

## The Lore: The Friday 4:45 PM Nightmare

It is 4:45 PM on a Friday. Your company’s VP of Sales just sprinted to your desk, visibly sweating. The board of directors called an emergency meeting for Monday morning to review regional revenue performance. 

The VP handed you a flash drive containing a single, completely corrupted, chaotic CSV file. 
* "The regional managers didn't use the CRM system," the VP stammers. 
* "They copy-pasted raw spreadsheets. The dates are in three different formats, currency symbols are mixed up, column headers change halfway down the file, and some entries look like they were typed by an angry toddler."

The VP needs actionable, clear, structured strategic insights on their desk in 15 minutes. You can't fix this file row-by-row. You need to build a prompting framework so resilient that it can ingest this digital toxic waste and spit out pure, board-ready gold.

---

## The Rules

1. **The Tech Stack is Wild West:** You are free to use any AI tool, model, or interface available to you (e.g., Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, Advanced Data Analysis, internal tools). Use whatever gets the best results the fastest.
2. **Absolute Time Box:** At the 15-minute mark, the submission window closes permanently. No extensions.
3. **Submission Portal:** You must submit your entry exclusively through the designated **Power App**.
4. **Deliverables:** To successfully complete your submission, you must provide exactly three items in the Power App fields:
   * **Enterprise ID**
   * **Prompt Text** *(The exact final prompt system/instructions you engineered)*
   * **Output Samples** *(The structured insights generated by your prompt from the provided messy data)*

## The Sample Data (The Mess)

Participants will be provided a raw, badly formatted CSV sample containing anomalies like:
* Dates written as `2026-05-26`, `26/05/05`, and `May 14th`
* Mixed currencies (`$5000`, `5000 INR`, `50k`)
* Missing fields, unmapped regional names, and broken rows.

## The Judging Matrix

Your submission will be evaluated by an **AI Automated Agent Reviewer** alongside human judges. The AI Agent will run your prompt against validation sets to test its absolute logic and structural resilience. 

### 🤖 The Agent Evaluation (The Machine Gatekeeper)
* **Clarity of Instructions:** How explicit, unambiguous, and authoritative are your prompt directives?
* **Structural Engineering:** Did you provide clear operational steps, explicit output formatting rules (e.g., JSON, clean tables), or few-shot samples within your prompt?
* **Output Relevance:** Did the prompt successfully force the AI to ignore the "noise" and focus purely on relevant metrics?
* **Logical Consistency:** Does the prompt hold up under pressure without contradicting its own instructions?

### 👤 The Human Evaluation (The Executive Review)
* **Insight Depth:** Did you surface underlying business trends, or just reformat the bad text?
* **Practical Usefulness:** Could the VP immediately copy-paste your output into a PowerPoint slide for the board of directors?
* **Handling Ambiguity:** How intelligently did your framework handle missing data fields and corrupted entries?
* **Decision Quality:** The logical soundness of the business conclusions drawn from the messy data pool.
